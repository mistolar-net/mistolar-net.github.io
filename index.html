Those of us blessed to know the Nivaclé have been moved by their cheerful and childlike simplicity, by their calmness in adversity and willingness to share, by their deep sense of community and attentive care for one another.  This site is dedicated to collecting and maintaining the unique and developing history of the Nivaclé communities at Mistolar, La Abundancia, and throughout the Chaco.

To view the histories, photos, and other materials which have been contributed to the site, click on the links following the names of their contributors listed below.  Copyrights belong to the individual contributors.

If you would like to add any photos, histories, accounts or journal entries to the site, please contact admin@mistolar.net.  We will scan photos and slides if needed, returning the originals along with digitized versions on DVD.  We can even digitize old cassette tape recordings you may have. 

Mistolar.net will feature English, Spanish, and Nivaclé translations as they become available.  Please let us know if you are a native Spanish speaker who can translate materials from English, and would like to volunteer your efforts.

Mistolar.net does not represent The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints or any other organization.
